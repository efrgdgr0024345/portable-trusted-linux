BLACK CAT LINUX — BRAND & DESIGN NOTES
Date: 16 September 2026

CORE TAGLINE
Stealth. Intelligence. Independence.

WHY THESE THREE WORDS MATTER

1. STEALTH — Visibility on your terms
Black Cat Linux is not about forcing users to hide.
It is about giving people deliberate control over how visible they are.

A user may choose to be:
- highly private
- anonymous or pseudonymous
- selectively visible to trusted people or services
- openly public
- fully auditable in selected areas

The system should make those choices understandable and intentional.

Example future modes:
- Hidden
- Private
- Trusted
- Public
- Transparent

A user could even choose to publish:
- lists of websites visited
- selected browsing/activity logs
- chosen application activity
- selected personal or project records

The principle is:
Stealth when you want it. Public when you choose it. Trusted either way.

Nothing should quietly change the user's visibility level without their knowledge.

2. INTELLIGENCE — Technology that strengthens human thought
The goal is not to create an operating system that thinks instead of people.

The goal is to create an operating system that helps people:
- think for themselves
- understand information
- create more freely
- explore ideas
- make their own decisions
- bring out human kindness, curiosity, creativity and love of life

AI should support human judgment rather than manipulate it.

The system should work for the user's interests, not for an advertising platform,
data broker, app-store gatekeeper or remote corporate agenda.

3. INDEPENDENCE — Build and act without permission
Black Cat Linux should reduce dependence on large centralized technology companies.

Users should be able to:
- build their own apps quickly
- create their own workflows
- create their own digital identities
- modify the system
- automate personal tasks
- choose their own services
- keep important capabilities local
- operate without needing approval from a large platform

The system should make software creation accessible even to people who are not
traditional programmers.

THE CENTRAL PHILOSOPHY

Privacy is not secrecy.
Transparency is not surrender.
The user controls the boundary.

A Black Cat Linux user should be able to choose exactly what the world sees,
understand what the system is doing, and build what they need.

SHORT BRAND STATEMENT

Stealth. Intelligence. Independence.

Choose what the world sees.
Understand what the system does.
Build what you need.

PROJECT DIRECTION

Black Cat Linux should become a trusted personal computing environment that:
- protects user agency
- reduces unnecessary tracking
- makes privacy understandable
- allows intentional public transparency
- supports local intelligence
- helps people create their own tools
- keeps the user, not the platform, in control

This document records the core branding and philosophy agreed on 16 September 2026.
